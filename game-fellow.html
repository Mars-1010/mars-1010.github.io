THE STARTING POINT

What makes
a teammate
feel present?

I started with a simple idea: a companion that stays alongside the player and joins in.

Before discussing the project with my teacher, I built small rule-based prototypes to explore following, fetching and shared tasks. The behaviour was hand-authored: a first way to test how a companion could appear to cooperate.

My teacher then recommended using Galaga as a concrete game setting. I moved the idea into a second ship, explored screenshot-based AI interpretation, and developed the work into a supervised research manuscript.

SCREEN-BASED AI PROTOTYPE

What can a teammate decide from the same screen the player sees?

I built a prototype that automatically sampled the game window at approximately 30 ms between captures. A vision model interpreted the screenshots to understand the game situation, including where things were on screen. That interpretation informed how the Fellow could help and what it said to the player.

The capture interval describes how often frames were sampled. It is separate from how long model analysis and the response take.

  1. 01 / CAPTURE

    Sample the screen.

    Automatically capture game-window screenshots roughly every 30 ms.

  2. 02 / INTERPRET

    Read the scene.

    Use a vision model to interpret the screenshot and reason about the current game situation.

  3. 03 / RESPOND

    Decide how to help.

    Use the interpretation to inform the companion’s next response, including spoken feedback.

The public browser demo is the rule-based version. It does not run screenshot analysis or make live model calls.

THE REPRODUCIBLE BUILD

Make support
legible.

The current Pygame controller makes the companion’s actions explicit, so they can be inspected and compared.

  1. 01

    Formation

    Maintain a stable position beside the player.

  2. 02

    Intercept

    Predict an incoming projectile from game-state positions and velocities, then move to protect the player.

  3. 03

    Support fire

    Coordinate a bounded follow-up shot with the player’s firing action.

  4. 04

    Recover

    Return to formation after supporting the player.

Short event-based callouts make support visible and audible. State transitions and support actions are logged alongside gameplay measures.

The game foundation is python-pygame-galaga by Patrick Kalkman (MIT). My work focuses on the companion, interaction behaviour, study flow and research framing.

MANUSCRIPT IN DEVELOPMENT

AI Fellows:
Toward Emotionally Attuned AI Teammates for Human–AI Co-Play

Research writing developed under academic guidance.

When does a game companion feel helpful, present and worth playing with?

The manuscript connects the system to questions about social presence, enjoyment, trust, motivation and game performance. I am interested in how players understand a companion’s actions, including moments when assistance becomes distracting or intrusive.

The current study preparation compares solo play with a balanced Fellow, using paired gameplay conditions and separate development logs. Human-participant evaluation is planned; these prototype demonstrations do not establish player-experience or performance benefits.
