01 / THE VISIONA DIRECTION TO DEVELOP THROUGH PHD RESEARCH

Understand me.
Think with me.
Make things happen.

A personal AI that becomes familiar through working together.

I imagine an AI that can follow an ongoing conversation while I work: understanding a request, noticing what I am looking at or changing, finding information, comparing possibilities and offering its own judgement. It can then help execute a plan through software and connected devices, observe the result and adjust with me.

Over time, it would remember relevant experiences, learn my habits and capabilities, and develop a consistent conversational presence. I could think aloud, revise an idea or return to an earlier project without rebuilding the entire context. It could also remind me, question an assumption or suggest another way forward.

Its presence might be a voice, a model we manipulate together, information appearing where it is useful, or a task completed in the background. XR interests me because it could bring this collaboration into shared space through spatial models, floating information and gestures. Design, immersive environments and games offer settings in which to explore it.

This is my long-term ambition. I would like to develop a tractable doctoral project within it through discussion with a supervisor, drawing on both working systems and empirical research.

02 / RESEARCH CONNECTIONUMANG BHATT / TRACE LAB

Personalised support

Dr Bhatt’s work on Learning Personalized Decision Support Policies offers a connection to my interest in assistance that develops around the individual. I would like to explore how an agent’s understanding of its user can evolve through collaboration and inform the support it provides.

Human–AI orchestration

TRACE Lab’s research on representing individual expertise and coordinating work around it connects with a central part of my vision: an AI that understands what its user brings to a task, contributes complementary capabilities and adapts their working relationship as circumstances change.

Evidence from actual use

The lab’s emphasis on evaluating AI in real settings is relevant to how I want to work. I would like to build prototypes and study repeated collaboration, examining task outcomes, the effort needed to establish shared understanding, and how people’s understanding and capabilities develop through AI use.

These are connections I would like to discuss. My interests in XR and spatial interaction could provide an experimental setting; the doctoral scope and supervision fit remain open for discussion.
