02 / RESEARCHPROPOSED PHD RESEARCH

Understand.
Act.
Continue together.

How can a personal AI understand what we are discussing in space, contribute an action, and pick up the work later?

I point to a display panel: “Turn this towards the entrance, but keep that work visible.” I sketch a route, walk around the model and change my mind. I want an AI that can follow this discussion, compare alternatives and help update the model.

My proposed starting point is an embodied assistant for a small exhibition workspace in XR. I would study how speech, pointing and drawing establish a shared understanding, and whether an editable working record helps connect that understanding to the next action. On a later visit, the assistant should help reconsider what has changed and why we made earlier choices.

For discussion with Associate Professor Selen Türkay: her team’s work on LLM-driven conversational agents in VR and drawing-supported spatial dialogue offers complementary starting points for this research.

The XR assistant and participant studies are proposed work. Game Fellow and the browser explorations document my preparation.
