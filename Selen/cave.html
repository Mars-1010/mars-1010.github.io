MY CONTRIBUTION

Working with
light & space.

At Fengyuzhu, I worked on the lighting, projection and spatial design of an immersive environment in 2023.

At Fengyuzhu, my responsibilities included lighting layout and calibration, projection content and equipment, on-site integration and spatial design. The CAVE project involved multi-channel projection fusion, wall-based touch control and radar recognition.

I’m now exploring gestures and body movement as ways to interact with this space: changing the light, opening another scene, or leaving a fleeting trace on the floor.

01 / SENSE

Hand & body tracking

MediaPipe detects 21 hand landmarks or 33 body landmarks. Position smoothing reduces jitter; mouse control remains available.

02 / INTERPRET

Interaction mapping

Hand and torso positions modulate projection brightness. A pinch and sideways pull opens the image; arm spread controls it in Body mode. Ground effects respond to the movement path.

position · pinch · arm spread
→
03 / RESPOND

Image & light

Cached exposure layers adjust the brightness of the stone’s existing projection. A split-and-warp effect opens the walls. Perspective-mapped lotus, water ripples and koi occupy the floor.

IMPLEMENTATION
Graphics
Local exposure masks brighten and dim the photograph’s existing projected textures. Ground-plane mapping places blooming lotuses, water ripples and animated koi on the floor. The wall reveal uses image splitting and warping.
On-device AI
MediaPipe Hand Landmarker or Pose Landmarker, loaded when camera tracking is requested. The model detects landmarks; mappings drive the scene.
Language
Preset scene commands with keyword matching. Optional browser speech-to-text. A general language model and generative video are future integrations.
Physical installation
Future: calibrated depth/radar tracking → local event service → TouchDesigner/Unity → projection, spatial audio and lighting via an appropriate OSC/DMX interface.
Spatial interface
The web interaction uses photographs. An XR extension would need a scene scan, calibrated tracking and a headset or AR implementation.


XR / EXTENDED REALITY
XR extensions
I’d like to extend these interactions into VR and AR/MR, with gestures linked to the geometry of the physical space.
VR
A virtual environment viewed through a compatible headset. Reconstructing the actual venue for VR requires additional 3D geometry and device validation.
AR / MR
Digital elements aligned with a physical place. A future visitor could reveal hidden layers of the cave with glasses or a phone.
Projection
A shared physical environment augmented by light. This project starts with spatial design, projection and sensing.
