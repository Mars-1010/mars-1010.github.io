02 / RESEARCHRESEARCH INTERESTS & INITIAL DIRECTIONS

Personal AI
for ongoing collaboration.

How can a personal AI understand an activity, reason with us, and help turn decisions into useful actions?

I want to explore an assistant that people can talk to while doing things: one that connects dialogue with the surrounding environment and digital artefacts, helps compare alternatives, and carries decisions into actions the person can inspect and revise.

Through repeated collaboration, it could become more familiar with a person's activities and preferences. I would study a recurring real-world activity where environmental context, dialogue and useful digital actions matter, with a wearable configuration that supports the person while they continue acting. Observation would guide the first activity and interaction design.

TOM, CRAFT and PersonaMail connect with this interest through situated wearable assistance, continuing creative work and context-sensitive adaptation. The proposal examines shared understanding, reasoning connected to action, and repeated collaboration during ongoing activities. My existing browser prototypes provide preparation; they do not implement or evaluate this proposed personal AI.
