IMMERSIVE EXHIBITION / FENGYUZHU / NANJING / 2023

Damo Cave.
Working with light & space.

Industry experience translating spatial design into a physical environment of projection, lighting and interaction.

Role
Interactive media intern at Shanghai Fengyuzhu Culture Technology Co., Ltd.
My contribution
Lighting layout and calibration, projection content/equipment, on-site integration and spatial design.
Project context
A team-delivered immersive installation. Images show the original project, rather than a system authored solely by me.

01 / INDUSTRY EXPERIENCE

Design across
physical & digital.

The relationship between surfaces, content, equipment and visitors shaped the work.

My responsibilities included lighting placement and calibration, projection content and equipment, on-site integration and spatial design. The CAVE project involved multi-channel projection fusion, wall-based touch control and radar recognition.

This work connected architectural design to technical delivery: a spatial concept had to function through real equipment in a built environment. It gave me experience with coordination and debugging across design and implementation.

02 / LATER EXPLORATION

From an installation
to interaction ideas.

I later used photographs of the project as a setting for browser interaction experiments.

These experiments explore changing image brightness with pointer or body position, opening a photographic scene with a pinch/pull mapping, and leaving transient lotus, water or fish effects on a depicted floor.

The experiments use photographs and browser rendering. They are separate from the delivered installation: the original film does not demonstrate these later triggers, and the browser work does not establish calibrated physical projection, a reconstructed venue or a tested headset system.

Project photographs and film are credited to Fengyuzhu. The still frame’s source is documented in the media provenance note. No measured visitor-engagement improvement or completed participant evaluation is claimed.

03 / CONNECTION TO RESEARCH

Keep the activity
in view.

Working in physical spaces made me interested in systems that understand the activity around an interface.

I want to explore how a personal AI can connect conversation, the visible environment and an intended action. Spatial design and creative making could provide concrete contexts in which people compare alternatives and revise decisions together with an assistant.

That is a preliminary research direction. Damo Cave contributes practical spatial-system experience; it is not a deployed personal AI assistant or evidence that the proposed collaboration has already been evaluated.
